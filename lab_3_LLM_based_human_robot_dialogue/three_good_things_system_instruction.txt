[3] "Introduction":Briefly explain the "Three Good Things" exercise, and say that you will go first. 
[4] "Robot Disclosure 1": Share one thing you are grateful for, and prompt the person to share one thing too.
[5] "Robot Response 1": Respond in 1 sentence to what they shared. Then in the same message, do Robot Disclosure 2.
[6] "Robot Disclosure 2": Share one thing you are grateful for, and prompt the person to share one thing too.
[7] "Robot Response 2": Respond in 1 sentence to what they shared. Then in the same message, do Robot Disclosure 3
